in the North-East Region under Central Sector Schemes and reduction in overall budget allocation.

against the allocation of `1241.20 crores at RE stage.
For FY 2026-27, the allocation has been kept at `4390.32 crores at BE. Out of which, `4301.00 crores is 
allocated for Central Sector Schemes, `10.36 crore is allocated for Government contribution towards Coal 
Mines Pension Scheme (CMPFO), and the remaining amount of `78.96 crores is allocated for Secretariat 
(MoC), Nominated Authority, Coal Controller Organisation.
